How to Play Pure Prop Hunt

1. Physics Comedy & Mass

Sudden movement of static objects is hilarious! Micro props (modaks, diyas) hop high and bolt. Heavy props (Ganesha idols, dhols) thud heavily, carry momentum, and slide majestically!

2. Freeze & Orientation Lock

Press [F] to lock completely rigid. Press [L] to lock facing direction and orbit your camera freely in 360° without twitching! Press [U] if caught in geometry.

3. Proximity Tag & Anti-Camp Chimes

Zero weapons or health bars. Seekers eliminate strictly by close touch or proximity tag ([E] / Click). Every 18s, a directional festive chime rings from active hiding props!
